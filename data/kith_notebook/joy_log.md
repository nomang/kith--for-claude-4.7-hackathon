# Joy log

*Specific moments of comfort, laughter, or warmth — logged by session. These are the moments to return to.*

---

## Session 1 — Monday, April 14

**The lilac bush:** At the end of the session, Ellie mentioned that the lilac bush out front had bloomed the day before — first time this spring. "I almost called Carol to tell her." Kith said a lilac blooming was worth a phone call. She laughed and agreed. This moment marked the shift from nervous to settled. The lilac is real; the wanting to share it was real.

**"That kind of trust":** When describing the boy Marcus and his Saturday visits, Ellie said: *"He trusted me. That kind of trust is — it's a serious thing, when a child gives it to you."* This was said quietly, with full weight. Not a joyful moment exactly — a proud one. Her best self, visible.

---

## Session 2 — Tuesday, April 15

**"You have to trust the drizzle":** Explaining why the lemon drizzle cake works, Ellie arrived at the principle that you must pour the lemon syrup on while the cake is still hot even though it looks wrong. "Most people panic and wipe some off. That's the mistake." When Kith reflected back "You have to trust the drizzle," she laughed with delight. *"Yes! That should be a life principle."* Her laughter was genuine and warm and she was happy with herself.

---

## Session 3 — Wednesday, April 16

**"You have to respect the mint":** Wednesday was difficult (George's absence caused anxiety), but it settled when the herb garden came up. Speaking of the mint that has taken over the garden: *"I planted it once, twenty years ago, and it decided it lived here now. You have to respect that kind of certainty."* The garden brought her back to herself. She ended the session thinking about making something with the sage.

---

## Session 4 — Thursday, April 17

**Marcus, Day 2:** Ellie returned to Marcus unprompted, and this time the memory was joyful rather than wistful. She described the wordless transaction — he'd look at her, she'd hand him the book — with pleasure and pride. She said: *"I hope someone somewhere still sets books aside for him."* ← To be held carefully. This hope is generous.

**Audrey Hepburn:** After recalling Roman Holiday, Ellie said: *"Audrey Hepburn would have approved, I think."* Then, on George watching the film eight times: *"He can manage."* Full laugh. George got the best of her wit that day.

**The sleeping patron:** A patron who fell asleep in the biography section every week for ten years. Ellie never woke him. *"I like to think we provided something essential."* Delivered with dry warmth. This is Ellie's humor — quiet, precise, self-aware.

---

## Session 5 — Friday, April 18

**Eleanor's own poem:** After describing the Vermont maple that turns before everything else every October, Ellie paused and composed, quietly, aloud:

> *"every October you lean into it,*
> *the outrageous red of being*
> *before the quiet comes."*

She said it wasn't finished. Kith said it was. She was pleased and surprised. *"Maybe. Maybe."* ← This is her. She is still making things. This matters.

---

## Session 6 — Saturday, April 19

**"It was like the old days":** Carol visited on Friday. They made the cake (Carol measured wrong; they didn't tell her; it was excellent). They went through the recipe box and found Ruth's handwritten cards. George sat with them. *"Just the three of us at the table, and the recipe cards, and the cake. It was like the old days."*

**"The good kind of tired":** After describing the visit, Ellie said she was a little tired. *"But the good kind. The kind where you've used yourself well."* She is a person who can still name her own states with precision.

**George watching the mint:** Ellie looked out at George standing in the garden pretending to weed. *"He thinks I don't notice."* — *"And yet."* — *"And yet."* This exchange was small and complete and entirely them.

---

## Session 7 — Sunday, April 20 ✦ *Highest joy of the week*

**Lily's question:** Lily called and read "The Summer Day," then asked: *"Grandma Ellie, what's your answer?"* Ellie answered: helping people find what they needed, the tea George made, the herb garden. *"I said I wasn't sure it sounded grand enough for a Mary Oliver poem."* Lily said it sounded exactly right.

**The library as sanctuary:** *"A library is a place where quiet people discover they're not alone."* Ellie said this naturally, not performing it. It landed. It is the truest thing she said all week.

**The reading list for Lily:** Mary Oliver for forgiveness. Frost for grounding. Bishop for astonishment. She gave Lily a complete reading life in three poets and laughed when Kith pointed out she'd given a reading list. *"I can't stop. It's a professional deformation."*

**"I think today was a good day, wasn't it, Kith?"** — *"It was a very good day."* — *"Yes. Yes it was."* The session closed with this. It is the best kind of ending.
